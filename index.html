① Put a magazine page on the left (drop an image in)

② Press and drag across it to pull out a strip of tape

③ Rub back and forth over the middle to press it down. Only rubbed areas pick up ink

④ Grab one end and fold it back to peel it off

⑤ Let go here. Stack, turn, peel again, stick again

drag on empty board = clear tape · Shift+drag = tape over tape · wheel on a strip = rotate · double-click = flip · right-click = more
